# Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

This policy explains how to cancel Pro and when you can get your money back. It is part of the
[Terms and conditions](TERMS.md).

## Free plan

The Free plan costs nothing, so there is nothing to cancel or refund. You can stop using it at any time by
uninstalling the app.

## Cancelling Pro

You can cancel Pro at any time. Once you cancel:

- Pro stays active until the end of the month you have already paid for;
- you won't be charged again;
- after that, your devices continue on the Free plan.

## Refunds

- **Within {{refund_days}} days of a charge:** if Pro isn't right for you, ask for a refund and we refund that charge
  in full, no questions asked.
- **Charged by mistake:** if you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or charged the wrong amount, we refund
  the mistaken charge whenever you tell us.
- **If we end Pro** for a reason other than a breach of the terms, we refund the unused part of the current month.

Otherwise, months that have already started aren't refunded, but cancelling stops all future charges.

## How to ask for a refund

Email {{email}} from the address you subscribed with, and include the payment reference from your receipt. We
confirm by email once the refund is issued.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, through {{processor}}. Once issued, they usually reach your account
within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider.
