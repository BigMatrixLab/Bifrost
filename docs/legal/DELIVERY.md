# Delivery policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Bifrost is software delivered over the internet. Nothing is shipped, and there are no delivery charges.

## What you receive

- **The app:** an installer for macOS (.dmg), Windows (.exe) or Android (.apk).
- **Pro:** a monthly subscription that changes how the app routes your transfers, as described in the
  [Terms and conditions](TERMS.md).

## Delivery of the app

The app is free to download from the [Download](site:#download) section of the website. The download starts at once
in your browser. Every installer has a matching .sha256 checksum on the same page, so you can confirm that the file
you received is genuine.

## Delivery of Pro

Pro is activated in the app as soon as {{processor}} confirms your payment, usually within a few minutes. A receipt
is sent to the email address you used. Each renewal is applied automatically, with a new receipt each month.

## If something doesn't arrive

If a download fails, start it again from the website: there is no limit on downloads. If Pro isn't active within an
hour of paying, or you didn't get a receipt, email {{email}} with your payment reference and we will sort it out. If
we can't deliver Pro, we refund the payment in full.
