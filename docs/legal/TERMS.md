## The service

Bifrost is an app for macOS, Windows and Android that sends files directly between devices, end-to-end encrypted.
It is offered on two plans, described on the [Pricing](site:#pricing) section of the website:

- **Free:** no charge. Your first 50 GB travel direct, device to device; after that every transfer goes through an
  encrypted relay. There is no limit on total data transferred.
- **Pro:** $5 per month. Transfers travel direct without the 50 GB switch; a relay is used only when routers block a
  direct path. There is no limit on total data transferred.

Both plans include the same encryption, block-by-block verification and privacy receipts.
